Privacy policy

The short version: the apps keep everything you enter on your phone. The only thing that uses the internet is the small ad at the bottom of the screen, which is shown by Google.

Last updated October 28, 2026. Applies to Tip or not?, Tuesday, Stance, Task Tracker, Comeback, $10M Ticket and One-Button Trio.

What the apps keep

Everything you type, save or score in an app (bills, tasks, quiz answers, game progress, settings) is stored only on your phone. FatSuit Apps has no servers, no accounts, and never receives or sees any of it. Deleting an app deletes its data.

  • Comeback uses your microphone to listen for insults. Speech is turned into text on your phone and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, including to the ad provider.
  • Task Tracker uses notifications for your reminders. They are scheduled on your phone.

Ads

The apps are free and show a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. The ads come from Google AdMob. When your phone is online, Google may collect and use some information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. That can include:

  • your phone's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location (from the IP address)
  • your phone model, operating system, language and which app is showing the ad
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Depending on your choices and where you live, Google may use this to show ads based on your interests. Google's own policy explains how: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

When your phone is offline, no ad is requested and the apps work as normal.

Your choices

  • In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, each app asks for your consent the first time you open it. You can change your answer at any time with the Ad privacy link under the ad.
  • On any Android phone, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Browser versions of Tuesday, Stance, $10M Ticket and One-Button Trio on this website show no ads.

Children

The apps are not made for children under 13, and FatSuit Apps does not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or ads. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
