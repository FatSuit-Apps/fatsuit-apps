Installing on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open the app.

Updating an app? Download the new version and install it over the one you have. Don't uninstall the old version first, or you could lose your saved data.
