How to install an app

It takes about a minute, and you do it all on your phone. Your phone will show a few warnings along the way. That's normal for any app that doesn't come from the Google Play Store. The steps below show you exactly what to tap.

What you need: an Android phone (for example Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola or OnePlus) with Android 8 or newer. Almost any phone from 2018 onwards will work.

Have an iPhone or a computer? The apps can't be installed there, but Tuesday and Stance also work in any web browser. Just tap their "in your browser" button.

  1. Open the FatSuit Apps website on your phone

    Open your phone's web browser (usually Chrome) and go to fatsuit-apps.github.io/fatsuit-apps, or tap the link someone sent you. If you're reading this on your phone, just tap Back to all apps.

  2. Tap the orange "Download for Android" button

    Pick the app you want and tap its orange button. Your phone may ask if you're sure:

    File might be harmful. Do you want to keep this file anyway?

    CancelDownload anyway

    Tap Download anyway.

  3. Open the downloaded file

    When the download finishes, tap Open in the message at the bottom of the screen.

    Missed it? Swipe down from the very top of your screen and tap the download. Or open the Files app (on Samsung: My Files) and look in Downloads.

  4. Give permission to install (first time only)

    The first time, your phone will stop you with a message like this:

    For your security, your phone currently isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source.

    CancelSettings

    Tap Settings, turn on the switch next to Allow from this source, then tap the back arrow to return.

  5. Tap "Install"

    Do you want to install this app?

    CancelInstall

    If a "Google Play Protect" message appears (for example "Unsafe app blocked"), tap More details, then Install anyway. If it asks to scan the app, you can tap Don't send. This only happens because the app isn't from the Play Store.

  6. Tap "Open". You're done!

    From now on, you'll find the app's icon with all your other apps.

    The first time you open them, Comeback asks to use your microphone (it needs to hear insults to answer them) and Task Tracker asks to send notifications (for your reminders). Tap Allow.

Getting a newer version later? Follow the same steps. At step 5 the button says Update instead of Install.

Never delete (uninstall) the old version first. Deleting it also deletes everything saved in it, like your tasks, bills or game progress.

Having trouble?
"App not installed"
Your phone may be short on space. Delete a few old photos or apps you don't use, then try again.
"There was a problem parsing the package"
The download may not have finished. Download it again. If it still happens, your phone may be older than Android 8.
Nothing happens when I tap the file
Open the Files app (on Samsung: My Files), go to Downloads and tap the file there.
